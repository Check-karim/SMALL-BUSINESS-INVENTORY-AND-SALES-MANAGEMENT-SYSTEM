About us

Brico NB Ltd keeps trade and home improvers supplied.

We sell tools, plumbing, electrical goods, paint, fasteners, and building materials from our Northbridge and Westford counters. The inventory system replaces the branch notebooks with one shared record.

Why the system exists

Stock used to live in separate books. A quotation at one counter could promise a product that another branch had already sold. Purchases were written up after the fact, so the shelf and the book drifted apart.

The system gives every branch the same catalogue, the same customers, and the same stock picture. When a sale is completed, the quantity at that branch comes down and the movement is kept.

How a purchase moves

  1. Check what is available at each branch.
  2. Prepare a quotation from that stock and those prices.
  3. Record the purchase, with the customer and the payment.
  4. The sale reduces stock and writes the movement.

The record

Names, usernames, and contact details stay clean.

People

A name uses letters and spaces. Numbers and special characters are refused.

Usernames

No spaces. The first character is a letter, so a username cannot start with a number or a special character.

Contact

Email must be a real address. A phone number must contain 10 to 15 digits. A new password must not be less than 6 characters.
